# Open rules questions for the user

One-line yes/no scenarios where the rules text left the engine a real choice. None from the
fuzzer; the card audit (349 cards) returned 2.

| # | Card / rule | Question | Engine does now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Akshan, Mischievous (two "when you play me" abilities) | When Akshan is played with the additional cost paid, may the controller order the gear steal after the Weaponmaster equip (instead of before)? | Steal first, then Weaponmaster (one chain item; the engine queues one item per trigger type per card) |
| 2 | Loyal Poro (718) | If Loyal Poro and the only other friendly unit at its battlefield die at the same time, does its Deathknell draw? | No |
| 3 | Death from Below (747) | Is its [A] replay from the trash made right away as the spell resolves, or is it a permission usable any time later that turn? | Usable for the rest of the turn |
